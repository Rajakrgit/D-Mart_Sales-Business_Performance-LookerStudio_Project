# D-Mart_Sales-Business_Performance-LookerStudio_Project
# 1. Project Overview
The Sales Performance Analytics project uses an interactive Looker Studio report to three interactive dashboard pages to review overall sales performance, product profitability, and sales-channel behaviour. It brings key business measures into one reporting experience and helps users explore performance by product, category, month, location, sales channel, and payment mode.
• Sales Performance Overview
• Product Performance
• Sales Channel Analysis

# 2. Business requirements
• Track key sales and profitability KPIs.

• Identify high-performing products and categories.

• Understand monthly and geographical sales patterns.

• Compare Direct Sales, Online, and Wholesaler channels.

• Explore payment behaviour using interactive filters.

# 3. Tools and technologies
• Primary tool: Google Looker Studio, Google Sheets and Microsoft Excel.

• Source data: sales, product, category, date, location, channel, and payment fields.

• Calculated metrics: sales amount, total profit, and profit margin, subject to the definitions used in the report.

• The displayed profit margin is 17.17%.

• Category04 has the highest displayed revenue contribution (23.7%); Category03 has the lowest (13.0%).

• Online and cash payments are almost evenly split.

• California is the leading location in the visible location-wise sales chart.

• Direct Sales has the largest bar in the channel performance chart.

# 6. Recommendations
• Review low-profit products and categories; assess cost, price, and demand.

• Track monthly sales against targets and investigate sudden changes.

• Compare channel profitability and operating costs, not sales alone.

• Study leading locations and identify opportunities in lower-performing areas.

• Add year-over-year growth, average order value, sales targets, and channel-wise profit.
